Our Story

Arete Fitness was created with the belief that luxury fitness can coexist with environmental responsibility. In the heart of New York, our club blends cutting-edge training with a deep commitment to sustainability. The facility runs on eco-powered equipment that feeds energy back into the grid, flooring made from recycled materials, and smart climate systems that reduce waste. Beyond the design, our culture is built on balance: high-performance training paired with wellness practices, and personal growth aligned with planetary health. At Arete Fitness, every rep, every breath, and every drop of sweat moves both you and the environment forward.
